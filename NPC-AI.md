# NPC AI
To extract simple standalone AI neural networks that an average CPU/GPU can handle so an NPC moves and behaves like a realistic sentient being.

## Extracting Neural Snippets
Work out a method to extract the core multidimensional neural pathways that make up a machine learning session and make them excecutable in a lightweight manner and usable within a game-engine.

## Model training
You would train each instance of an NPC separately, from a normal humanoid body, to an 8-legged giant tarantula, to a snail. Each with different bodies, limbs, personalities etc. So each NPC would have their own AI-snippet.

# Movement System

## Model Movement
One of the primary goals is to remove the well-known robotic changing between different animations of an NPC. While there are existing ways to smoothen, or tween between different animations; between attacks; running; stopping etc. this still has a rigid modular approach to NPC actions.
The real goal is to move its limbs individually, but the body as a whole with it; this should be the primary training goal, to have sensible body movement for different actions.

## Brain first approach
In animal movement, the head is primary and always the most important to protect, and to move around to see or hear their surroundings, or even taste or smell it. The 4 primary sense are all concentrated in one place.
