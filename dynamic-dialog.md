# Dynamic Dialog

LLM-powered dialog generation.


## Real-time conversing


### Choice Pauses
When user is deciding what to answer, the NPC(s) would fill in the space realistically in several stages, depending on how long the response takes:
1. Silence, or depending on the urgency, non-verbal gesturing.
2. Depending on the context, the other(s) might grow increasingly inpatient, awkward or disappointed etc.
3. Increased furstration, uncertainty, or confusion will cause the NPCs to verbally try to get the player to answer or respond.
4. Potential consequences of inaction (depnding on how the conversation is configured or limited), a termination of the conversation on the NPC side, or even a complete acceptance and surrender of the situation to the player.
